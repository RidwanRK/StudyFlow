StudyFlow

An AI study planner for university students. It builds a day-by-day timetable before exams, and when a student falls behind, it proposes a replan and explains what moved and why.

From scope to product

Scope said

"The AI replans the remaining schedule and shows what moved and why."

Now we know

After the student marks the day, they choose Replan or Move forward. Replan shows proposed changes with reasons, and the student accepts or rejects them. Move forward leaves the plan alone and shows a "remaining" alert.

The core journey — click a step, or use Next

The replan beat

The "oh, that's cool" moment. Example: Tuesday's session was only half done, the student clicks Replan, and sees this before anything changes.

BeforeTue — Biology Ch. 4 (2h)
Wed — Chemistry Ch. 2 (2h)
Thu — Biology Ch. 5 (2h)
ProposedTue — Biology Ch. 4 (1h done)
Wed — Chemistry Ch. 2 (2h)
Thu — Biology Ch. 4 remainder (1h) ⇠ moved, Ch. 5 (1h)
"Chapter 4 moved to Thursday because Tuesday's session was only half done."

Details

  • Login page — Register / Sign in with username and password. First thing anyone sees.
  • Welcome message — first login only; explains what StudyFlow does.
  • Setup: study hours — weekday hours, weekend hours, per-day overrides (e.g. Sunday).
  • Setup: courses — course name and exam date, then topics each with difficulty and hours. A hint says "if not sure, take a safe guess". Generate plan button.
  • Plan view — day-by-day list to the last exam; each day collapses/expands into its detailed timetable. Today is expanded on return.
  • Check-in — per item: completed / partial (hours studied, optional "still need more time") / untouched. Then Move forward or Replan.
  • Replan proposal — proposed changes with "what moved and why", Accept / Reject.
  • Full-screen, responsive: same experience on phone and desktop.
  • First use — welcome message, then setup. No plan until Generate plan.
  • Returning — saved data; today's day expanded, others collapsed.
  • Unknown hours for a topic — hint tells the student to take a safe guess; never blocked.
  • Hours don't fit before the exams — suggestion: add x hours daily, or y extra on one or two days. Accept → plan built with the change. Reject → lighter plan, a kind note that they may not finish everything, and encouragement to cover the important parts.
  • Partial/untouched, moving forward — shows as a "remaining" alert; no replan.
  • Login problems — assumption: short, friendly message for wrong password or taken username.
  • Look and feel — playful but soothing, lightly colored, always consoling and confident with the student.
  • I can add several courses, each with one exam date and several topics with difficulty and hours.
  • The hours field shows the "safe guess" hint.
  • I can set weekday and weekend hours and change a single day. No day in the plan exceeds its hours.
  • Generate plan shows a day-by-day list to my last exam; each day expands to its timetable.
  • Every topic is scheduled before its own course's exam, with earlier exams and harder topics first.
  • Each day's timetable shows hour blocks.
  • I can edit courses, topics, hours, exam dates or daily hours later, and the plan adjusts.
  • Each item can be marked completed / partial / untouched; partial asks only for hours studied and shows what's left.
  • Replan shows proposed changes plus "what moved and why" before anything changes.
  • Accept updates the timetable; Reject leaves it unchanged.
  • Move forward shows a "remaining" alert and leaves the plan unchanged.
  • When time doesn't fit: I see the add-hours suggestion; accepting rebuilds the plan; rejecting gives a lighter plan with a consoling message.
  • On my next login, today's plan is expanded and the other days are collapsed.
