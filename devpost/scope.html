StudyFlow (working name)

An AI study planner for university students that builds a day-by-day timetable before exams, then replans automatically when the student falls behind.

The unique kernel

The AI does the planning and the replanning. When something isn't finished, the plan adjusts itself and shows a short "what moved and why" note, for example "Chapter 4 moved to Thursday because Tuesday's session was only half done." The student can see and trust the AI's reasoning.

Who it's for

University students with a lot of material to cover before exams. Today it's hard for them to keep a timetable going, and a plan made once falls apart the first time a day goes wrong.

The core loop

They come back each day because the plan is always current, and falling behind doesn't mean starting over.

The steps in words

  1. The student logs in and enters courses, what to cover, difficulty, exam dates and required hours.
  2. The AI builds a detailed timetable.
  3. After each day the student marks items completed, partially completed or untouched.
  4. For partial items, the system asks only how many hours were studied and works out what's left. An optional "still need more time on this" tick covers the rest.
  5. The AI replans the remaining schedule and explains what moved and why.

What "working" looks like

A student logs in with a username and password, enters their courses and exam details, and gets a timetable. They mark a day as partly done and enter the hours studied. The plan changes in front of them, with a clear note on what moved and why. It works on both phone and desktop.

The "oh, that's cool" beat: the timetable visibly reshuffles itself and explains why.
